# Coca-Cola_Sales_Analysis
Coca-Cola Sales Analysis
Project Overview
This project analyzes Coca-Cola sales performance across products, regions, retailers, and months to identify revenue drivers, profitability patterns, and opportunities for business improvement.
The project is designed as an end-to-end Data Analyst portfolio project and demonstrates practical Excel skills such as data organization, KPI analysis, aggregation, business interpretation, and dashboard-style reporting.
Dataset note: The included dataset is synthetic training data created for portfolio and interview practice. It is not official Coca-Cola company data.

Business Problem
A beverage company needs to understand:
Which products generate the most revenue?
Which regions perform best?
Which retail channels contribute the most sales?
How does sales performance change month to month?
Which areas should management prioritize?
How can revenue growth be balanced with profitability?

Project Objectives
Analyze total sales and profitability.
Compare product performance.
Compare regional performance.
Evaluate retailer/channel performance.
Identify monthly sales trends.
Calculate business KPIs.
Convert analysis into actionable recommendations.

Key KPIs
KPI	Definition
Total Revenue	Sum of sales revenue
Total Cost	Total estimated product cost
Total Profit	Revenue − Cost
Profit Margin	Profit ÷ Revenue
Units Sold	Total units sold
Average Revenue / Order	Total revenue ÷ number of transactions

Key Findings
Based on the included analysis:
Coca-Cola is the leading product by revenue.
South is the leading region by revenue.
Supermarket is the strongest retail channel.
Apr is the highest-revenue month.
Overall profitability is approximately 39.0%.
These findings are based on the synthetic training dataset included in this repository.

Business Recommendations
1. Prioritize high-performing products
Maintain strong inventory availability for the leading products and investigate promotional opportunities.
2. Strengthen regional performance
Study successful practices in the strongest region and assess whether they can be replicated elsewhere.
3. Optimize retail channels
Use channel-specific pricing, promotions, and distribution strategies instead of treating every retailer equally.
4. Improve demand planning
Use monthly sales patterns to support inventory and campaign planning.
5. Monitor profitability
Revenue alone is not enough. Track profit margin by product, region, and retailer.
